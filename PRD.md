# Product Requirements Document — FinSight AI

## Problem
Finance/MIS teams spend significant time turning ERP exports into management commentary. The recurring workflow combines data validation, budget-vs-actual analysis, cost-driver identification, anomaly review, forecasting and action tracking.

## Users
- FP&A / Finance Analyst
- Finance Manager / Business Controller
- Product/Project Manager supporting delivery economics

## User stories
1. As a Finance Analyst, I can ask a business question in plain English and receive evidence-backed analysis.
2. As a Finance Manager, I can identify the largest unfavorable variances by business unit and cost category.
3. As a PM, I can turn material financial risks into prioritized actions with owners and due dates.
4. As a stakeholder, I can see charts and KPIs instead of reading raw ERP rows.

## MVP scope
Natural-language question → tool routing → SQL/ML analysis → visual evidence → executive summary → action backlog.

## Success metrics
- Reduce manual analysis steps from raw data to management commentary.
- Return top cost drivers consistently and reproducibly.
- Ensure every narrative statement can be traced to a tool result.
- Produce action owners and deadlines for material variances.

## Out of scope
Production ERP integration, real corporate credentials, autonomous journal posting, autonomous financial decisions.
